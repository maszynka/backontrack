01 — The Problem & The Solution

We open a browser
and forget why

The average person spends 7+ hours daily online. Most of that time is unintentional — we open a tab with purpose, get pulled into a feed, and surface 45 minutes later having accomplished nothing. BackOnTrack intervenes at the critical moment.

The Problem

Modern websites are engineered to capture attention. Users lose track of intent the moment they arrive.

  • Infinite scroll patterns hijack dopamine loops
  • Auto-playing content derails focus
  • No mechanism exists to check your own intent
  • Hours lost to unconscious browsing daily

The Solution

BackOnTrack creates a pause — a micro-intervention that restores intentionality to browsing.

  • Prompts you to declare your intent on arrival
  • Reminds you of that intent after a set time
  • Visually dissociates the page to break the trance
  • Lightweight, non-intrusive, fully user-controlled

03 — Core Modules

Two engines, one mission

BackOnTrack consists of two complementary modules that work together to snap you back to conscious browsing.

?

"Why Am I Here?" (WYAH)

The cognitive module. Injects a minimal input form into any webpage via Shadow DOM. You declare your reason for visiting, set a countdown timer, and the extension reminds you of your stated purpose when time expires.

Intent Capture → Timer → Reminder ~

Dissociation Effect

The perceptual module. Applies a visual distortion effect to the entire page content, creating a CSS-driven "dissociation" that makes the page feel unfamiliar — breaking the autopilot state and forcing conscious re-engagement.

Visual Disruption → Break Autopilot

05 — Technology

Built with web standards

No frameworks, no build dependencies beyond Sass. BackOnTrack is intentionally lightweight — pure JavaScript injected directly into the DOM via Chrome's extension APIs.
